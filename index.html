FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do customers need to download an app?

No. They just scan the QR — everything opens in the browser. No app, no signup.

Are AI reviews safe? Will Google flag them?

Every review is generated uniquely, and the customer copies and posts it themselves — we never auto-post anything. That's what keeps it safe.

What does an unhappy customer do?

Instead of a 1-star Google review, they send you private feedback that only appears in your dashboard — so you can fix it.

How do I pay?

Simple UPI transfer. After paying, just tell us on WhatsApp and we'll activate your plan.

What if I don't pay?

You get a 3-day grace period with reminders; after that, the QR pauses. It resumes the moment you renew.

I'm not good with computers…

No problem — the entire dashboard works on your phone. From QR download to payment, everything is mobile.
